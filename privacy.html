Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026. This policy covers the Agenda app for Android (package uk.jj.agenda) and this website. Agenda is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • Everything you put into Agenda is kept on your phone, encrypted. It is never uploaded, to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Agenda does not hold Android's internet permission. It cannot go online, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics.
  • Copies leave the app only when you make them: a backup, an export or a shared project.

What stays on your phone

Agenda keeps your tasks, projects, sections, labels, notes, reminders, saved places, filters, focus sessions, completed history and settings in a database in the app's private storage. The database is encrypted with SQLCipher (AES-256) using a random key wrapped by Android's Keystore. Attached photos, voice memos and files are encrypted separately with AES-256-GCM. A copy taken off the phone cannot be read. The PIN is not stored: only a salted, one-way hash of it.

Photos you read tasks from are read on the phone by a text reader built into the app. Taking a photo uses your phone's own camera app.

Backups, exports and sharing

  • Backups hold everything, including attached files, encrypted with a key made from your backup password. Without the password they cannot be opened, by anyone. Automatic backups go to the one folder you picked; if it is synced to a cloud service, that service's terms apply to the copy.
  • Exports (PDF, spreadsheet, text, calendar or JSON) are not encrypted. They leave out attached files.
  • Shared projects hold that project's open tasks only: no history, files or other projects.

Widgets and notifications

The Today widget shows today's tasks. With the app lock on, it shows only how many there are. Reminder notifications show the task's name, as you would expect.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. Deleted tasks wait in the Bin for 30 days. Settings, Your data, Erase everything removes it all. Uninstalling Agenda deletes everything the app holds. Backups and exports you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands.
