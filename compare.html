What Agenda does not do

Agenda has no syncing between devices, no web or desktop version, no shared workspaces and no AI assistant, because it never goes online. It is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site and install new versions over the old one.

Where the others do more

Todoist and TickTick sync across phones, tablets, computers and the web, let several people work in the same projects, and connect to calendars and other services. Both update themselves from their app stores.
